Privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

BandNine is an app for practising the IELTS Speaking test with a live examiner. This page sets out what the app stores, who processes it and how you delete it. If anything here is unclear, write to alisahindev@gmail.com.

Your account

When you open the app for the first time, it creates an anonymous account for you. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number. The account is a random identifier stored on your phone and on our server. It holds your target band, your exam date if you give one, your feedback language, your credit balance and whether you have Plus.

Your speaking recordings

During a practice test your voice is streamed to the examiner so it can listen and reply. The app saves a recording of each of your answers so that you can play it back and so that Pronunciation can be estimated. Recordings are kept for 90 days and then deleted automatically. Only your account can open them.

Transcripts and band estimates

The app keeps a written transcript of each test and the estimated bands and feedback it produces. These are AI practice estimates, not official IELTS results. They stay in your account so you can see your progress, until you delete the account.

Who processes your data

The examiner and the scoring run on Google Gemini. Your voice, transcripts and recordings are sent to Google to produce the examiner's replies and your feedback, and are not used by us for anything else.

Your account, transcripts, estimates and recordings are stored with Supabase in Paris, France.

Purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play. RevenueCat records which purchases belong to your anonymous account so that credits and Plus reach the right place. We never see your card details.

The app sends usage events to PostHog, hosted in the EU, so we can see which parts of the app work and which do not: for example, that a test started or finished, how long the examiner took to reply, or that a screen was opened. Events carry your anonymous identifier. They never contain your recordings or transcripts.

We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising.

Deleting your account

Open the You tab in the app and tap Delete account at the bottom. This removes your recordings, transcripts, band estimates and account straight away. It cannot be undone. Deleting the app from your phone does not delete the account on our server, so delete the account first.

Children

BandNine is meant for adults and older teenagers preparing for IELTS. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions and requests go to alisahindev@gmail.com.
